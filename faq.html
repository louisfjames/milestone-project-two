FAQ

Everything you need to know about how FestFind works

FestFind currently uses the Skiddle API to pull in real‑time festival data, including dates, locations, and lineups. We combine this with additional trusted sources to keep everything accurate and up to date. Once the data comes in, we format it so you can browse festival info quickly, clearly, and without the usual hassle.

You can search by artist, festival name, or city. Type in whatever you’re looking for and we’ll instantly load key details — including dates, locations, lineups, and links to explore further. It’s designed to help you find the right festival without any hassle.

The Festival Spotlight is randomly generated each time you visit the Discover page. It’s our way of surfacing something unexpected — a festival you might not have searched for but could end up loving. Think of it as a little dose of curated chaos.

FestFind is an independent online project created for festival fans. We’re not tied to any promoter or ticketing company and we just want to make festival discovery simple, transparent, and fun. Everything on the site is built with the user experience front and centre.

We use live data sources, update frequently, and prioritise accuracy across the site. Every festival page is designed to be clear, consistent, and easy to understand, so you always know where the information comes from and what you’re looking at.
